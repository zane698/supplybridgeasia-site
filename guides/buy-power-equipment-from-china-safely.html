Guide

How to buy generators and transformers from China without getting burned

Most problems with Chinese power equipment are not about the factory's ability. They come from unclear specifications, payment made too early and evidence that nobody checks. This checklist covers what protects you.

1. Fix the specification in writing

The quotation or proforma invoice should state the exact rating (prime and standby for generators, kVA and voltages for transformers), engine and alternator models, controller, winding material (copper or aluminium), standard, tests included, warranty and lead time. If a line says "brand to be confirmed", ask before you pay.

2. Link payment to evidence

  • A deposit (typically 30%) starts production.
  • The balance is paid after you have seen the factory test: report, photos with serial numbers and, for generators, the set running on a load bank by live video.
  • Avoid paying 100% before production unless the order is small and the supplier is well known to you.

3. Protect yourself against bank-detail fraud

The most common loss in international trade is not a bad factory but a fake email asking you to pay a "new" bank account. Rules that stop it:

  • The bank account should appear only on the contract and proforma invoice.
  • The beneficiary name should match the company you signed with.
  • Any change of bank details must be confirmed by a live video or voice call with the person you have been dealing with, never only by email.

4. Check the company, not only the website

  • Ask for the business licence and the company's customs registration (exporters in China have one).
  • Ask for a WhatsApp video call, and a live video from the factory floor showing your type of equipment.
  • Ask for sample type-test and routine-test reports for the product family.

5. Use a third-party inspector for larger orders

BV, SGS or Intertek can witness the factory test and check quantity, nameplates and packing for a day rate. Some countries require a pre-shipment inspection certificate, so tell your supplier the destination early.
