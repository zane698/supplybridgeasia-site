Why buy through us

One contract, the right factory for each item

Transformers, switchgear and generator sets are built by different specialist manufacturers. We bring them into one order and take responsibility for it, from specification to delivery.

Quotation and contract

The whole package on one contract with our company: scope, price, delivery and payment terms in writing, one invoice, one shipment.

Specification check

We check models, ratings, materials and drawings, and bring every deviation to you for approval.

Inspection and documents

We organise the inspection scope, share the evidence, and coordinate packing and export paperwork.

Warranty and claims

12-month warranty, written into the sales contract. Send the order number and photos; we handle the claim with the manufacturer.

Setting us up as a supplier

We can provide registration documents and the information your procurement team needs. Ask by email and tell us which forms you use.
