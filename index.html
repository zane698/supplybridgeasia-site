How an order works

You see the evidence before you pay the balance

Send your requirement

Rating, voltage, quantity, destination and deadline. A single-line diagram or specification helps.

Quote within two working days

Price, technical data sheet, applicable test reports and a lead time confirmed against the manufacturer's schedule.

See the factory before you commit

On request we send a recorded video from the factory showing the production line and equipment of your specification.

Contract and deposit

The sales contract and proforma invoice state the specification, inspection scope, acceptance criteria, warranty and payment schedule. You pay our company account.

Production and factory tests

Production photos at agreed stages. Routine tests witnessed live by video, or by your own third-party inspector (SGS, BV, Intertek or your nominee).

Balance, then shipment

You review the test report, photos and video, then pay the balance. Original documents or telex release follow cleared payment.

How you pay

Bank transfer (T/T) to our company account: deposit with the order, balance after you review the inspection evidence and before shipment. Sight L/C may be considered for larger orders. The exact schedule is in each proforma invoice.

Trial orders welcome

Start with one unit and check the quality before a larger order.

Bank details never change by email

Our account is stated only on the contract and proforma invoice. If you receive a change notice, call us before paying.
