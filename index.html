FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

Are you a manufacturer?

No. We are a sourcing and export company. Your contract and invoice are with our company; we select the manufacturer, check the specification, organise testing and inspection, and coordinate export. If anything goes wrong, you deal with us.

How fast do you quote?

We typically reply within 24 hours. Standard ratings are often quoted the same day. Packages built to a single-line diagram can take longer, and we tell you when to expect them.

What is the minimum order?

One unit. Trial orders are welcome.

What warranty do you give?

12 months, written into the sales contract. Send the order number and photos and we handle the claim with the manufacturer.

Which prices do you quote: FOB or CIF?

FOB Shanghai, Qingdao or Ningbo as standard. We can quote CFR or CIF to your port on request.

Can I inspect before shipment?

Yes. Routine tests can be witnessed by live video, and your own third-party inspector is welcome. You pay the balance only after you have reviewed the results.

Can you supply 60 Hz or other voltages?

Yes. Tell us the voltage, frequency and standard you need and we confirm it in the quote.
